# Changelog

## 2026-07-10

- Added a light theme. Commandle now follows your device's light/dark
  preference, and you can pick System, Light or Dark from the settings menu.
- Softened the glowing background flames so pages are calmer and easier to
  read.
- Game tiles on the All Games page lost their orange side-stripe; the accent
  now lives in each tile's icon and hover glow.
- Panels on the account pages are now solid instead of frosted glass —
  crisper text, and a bit faster on older phones.
- Section labels and column headers are no longer ALL CAPS; they now use
  regular title casing and are slightly larger for readability.
